# Session 10 — Human correction to a proposed structured lesson

## Goal
Capture a controller correction with cited evidence, optionally apply a human
proposal without calling the investigator, and ask the provider for a
`wire_fee_lookup_v1` draft only. Do not activate lessons or add a second
memory store.

## Completed changes
- `open_evidence`, `save_correction`, `apply_correction`, and `propose_lesson`
  are public service seams. UI/CLI must use them rather than a second pathway.
- Saving a correction creates a HUMAN run, opens cited documents, validates an
  optional proposal, and records eligibility. It does not mutate balances.
- Applying a correction reopens evidence in a fresh HUMAN context and posts
  through `apply_proposal` with actor `controller`/`demo_controller`.
- The compiler uses `lesson_compiler.md` plus `propose_precedent` /
  `cannot_generalize`. At most two model requests and a 60-second deadline.
  Failed compiles leave the correction and do not fabricate a draft.
- Host checks reject write-offs, widened customer scope, executable
  instructions, and scope that is not the verified source. Drafts stay
  `DRAFT` and are not inserted into `workspace_memory`.
- CLI: `precedent correction save|apply` and `precedent lesson propose --live`.
  `--live` never selects `ScriptedProvider`.

T03 IDs used: case `CASE-1CFA9FEE848F`, payment `PAY-201`, remittance
`DOC-R201`, fee notice `DOC-F201`, invoice `INV-1042`.
