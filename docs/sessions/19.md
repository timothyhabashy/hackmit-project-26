## Completed changes

**Consolidation 1: one reconciliation.** `ui_queue._reconcile` and
`ui_case._reconcile` were separate implementations of the same question with
the same names and different shapes. Both are now
`src/precedent/ui_reconcile.py`. It kept the two genuinely different readings
of "what was billed" instead of collapsing them: `invoice_outstanding_cents`
(what the remittance-named invoices still owe now, which the queue asks) and
`invoice_original_cents` (what the candidates were billed, which the fee
equation needs after the invoice is paid to zero). Verified behavior is
preserved: the queue shows blank rather than `$0.00` when no remittance
matches, an already-applied payment reports `$0.00` unexplained through
`settled` rather than subtraction, and Case Detail falls back to the payer's
full invoice list and says so.

**Consolidation 2: promoted into `ui_theme`.** `escape_text` (was the private
`_text`, copied as `_escape` and `_esc` in three modules), `state_text`,
`quiet`, `body`, `bullets`, `card_title`, `chip`, `note_block`,
`budget_strip` with `BudgetReading`, `count_strip`, `lifecycle_stepper` with
its four state tables, and `equation_block` / `chain_block` /
`verdict_block`. The `.pc-eq-*`, `.pc-chain-*`, and `.pc-verdict-*` rules
moved from `ui_case._CASE_STYLESHEET` into the central layer, which is now
the only stylesheet the app injects. New shared classes replaced inline
style strings: `.pc-strip*`, `.pc-step*`, `.pc-note*`, `.pc-body`,
`.pc-list*`, `.pc-card-title*`, `.pc-tag*`.

**Consolidation 3: page rename finished.** `app_pages/learning_results.py` is
`app_pages/lessons.py` at `/lessons`, with `PAGE_LEARNING_PATH` and the test
constant `LEARNING_PAGE` updated together. `/lessons` was confirmed to
deep-link in a browser.

**Copy pass.** `st.caption` went from 84 in the original `ui.py` (against 1
title and 10 subheaders) to 22 across all six UI modules, and every survivor
is genuine fine print: identifiers, content hashes, oracle values, and
disclaimers. Everything else moved to the tier it belonged in.

**Em-dash sweep.** Zero remain in `src/precedent/ui*.py`, `app_pages/`,
`tests/test_app.py`, `README.md`, `docs/BUILD_STATE.md`,
`docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`, and `docs/RESULTS.md`. The
recorded-run label is now `Recorded run - no live model calls` in
`ui_results.RECORDED_RUN_LABEL`, in the `tests/test_app.py` assertion, and in
every doc that quotes it.

**Accessibility.** Three fixes and three confirmations, see below.

**Two defects found and fixed during browser QA.** At 1024px the five queue
metrics compressed to 101px each, truncating four labels and rendering the
headline as `$65...`; the row now wraps. And `st.Page(..., url_path=...,
default=True)` silently ignores `url_path`, so `/work-queue` opened a "Page
not found" dialog before falling back; the dead declaration is removed and
Work Queue is documented as living at `/`.

Contrast fix: `FAINT` moved from slate-500 `#64748B` to `#5B6A80`.
Slate-500 measured 4.34:1 on the `#F1F5F9` recessed surface, which is where
this tier actually lands inside gap cells, document headers, equation
headers, the timeline, and the app context strip. The `LIVE UNAVAILABLE`
provenance chip was the worst real instance. It now measures 5.02:1 there and
5.50:1 on white.

Disabled-control fix: Streamlit fades a disabled button below readable
contrast. Disabled buttons are load-bearing here, since they are how the app
says a chargeable live call cannot be made, so they now render at the muted
tier with a dashed border and a not-allowed cursor. Shape and cursor, not
color alone, mark them unavailable.

Verified in the browser: T03 and T06 both read $9,965.00 received /
$10,000.00 invoiced / $35.00 unexplained on a fresh ledger; T06's evidence
chain ends in an amber "Nothing references ST-276395BFD4E3" step while T03's
closes on `DOC-F201`; resolved T03 reads "Explained difference $35.00" with
the application cited; the unmatched case renders blank with "No remittance
on file"; Candidate invoices shows one row, not fourteen; a dataframe row
click and the `case_select` control stay in sync in both directions.
